Case Study 1

Building a Scalable Data Foundation for Enterprise Sales

Creating a trusted operating layer for reporting, decision-making, and future AI enablement.

Problem

Sales and leadership teams operated across fragmented systems, which limited visibility, reduced trust in reporting, and slowed decision-making.

Approach

Led the strategy and execution of a centralized data foundation to unify and govern critical business data across CRM and downstream systems, enabling a more reliable source of truth for operational and executive use cases.

Key Actions

  • Defined a scalable data ingestion and governance framework across multiple systems
  • Aligned stakeholders across Sales, IT, and Data Engineering on data definitions and priorities
  • Established standardized reporting layers to support both operational execution and leadership visibility

Impact

  • Improved consistency and reliability of reporting across cross-functional sales operations
  • Enabled faster, more confident decision-making for leadership
  • Created a scalable, AI-ready data foundation to support advanced analytics, automation, and future intelligent decisioning

Case Study 2

Transforming CRM Data Quality to Improve Sales Effectiveness

Turning low-trust CRM data into a more usable business asset.

Problem

Inconsistent and incomplete CRM data reduced confidence in reporting, impacted sales productivity, and introduced downstream operational inefficiencies.

Approach

Designed and implemented a data quality framework and reporting layer to monitor, improve, and operationalize CRM data integrity across the sales organization.

Key Actions

  • Developed data quality metrics and dashboards to track completeness, accuracy, and source attribution
  • Identified upstream workflow gaps and partnered with stakeholders to improve data capture processes
  • Embedded quality reporting into business cadences to increase visibility, accountability, and adoption

Impact

  • Increased awareness and adoption of data quality standards across sales teams
  • Improved pipeline visibility and reporting accuracy for leadership
  • Strengthened the quality of inputs used for segmentation, predictive insights, and AI-enabled decision support

Reducing system noise while improving platform health and data usability.

Problem

Approach
